# Owner dashboard

The owner's job is to maintain the actual bilingual menu and review a draft before publishing.

Use the existing brand palette: black #050505, charcoal #18181A, elevated surface #222224, white #F7F4F2, red #E51F2A, muted text #ABA5A2. Use Alexandria for body and Arabic and Barlow Condensed for the 10:10 wordmark and prices.

The searchable product list is the central workspace. A quiet sidebar selects Menu, Categories, Website content, and Contact. The top bar always distinguishes Save draft, Preview, and Publish. An editorial strip showing a hookah, juice, and coffee from the actual collection anchors the owner UI in this café instead of generic analytics.

Use native dialogs for editing and confirmation, explicit status labels, large mobile controls, visible focus, keyboard category-reordering buttons, and reduced-motion support. The live menu remains unchanged until Publish.
